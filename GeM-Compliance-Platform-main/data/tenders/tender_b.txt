CPCL/PROC/2026/002 — Sample CPCL Procurement Tender (Tender B)

Eligibility Criteria:

1. Minimum Average Annual Turnover: The bidder must have an average annual
   turnover of at least INR 3 Crore over the last three financial years,
   supported by audited financial statements.

2. GST Registration: The bidder must hold a valid and active GST
   registration (GSTIN).

3. PAN: The bidder must possess a valid Permanent Account Number (PAN).

4. Udyam Registration: The bidder must have an active Udyam (MSME)
   registration.
